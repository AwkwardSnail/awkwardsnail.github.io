Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

> short version

Our apps do not collect, store or send any personal information.

> details

  • No accounts or sign-ins.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • Anything you create in an app stays on your device, unless you choose to move it yourself.
  • The apps never contact our servers, and they work offline unless going online is their job.

The one app that goes online is TextBrowser, because it is a web browser. It connects only to the websites you visit, and to DuckDuckGo Lite when you search. Those sites see your visit as they would in any browser. The Windows version loads pages with Microsoft's WebView2 engine, which follows your Windows diagnostic data settings.

When you buy an app, the store (for example the Microsoft Store) handles the payment under its own privacy policy. We don't receive your payment details.

This website has no cookies or analytics. Its fonts load from Google Fonts, and it is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs.

> changes and contact

If an app ever needs to handle data, we will update this page first and say so in the app's description.
